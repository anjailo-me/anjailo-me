# Profile assets

The Windows-inspired header and surrounding icon tiles are original profile artwork.

JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Node.js and Electron logo paths come from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), under its MIT license. The .NET logo comes from the official [.NET brand repository](https://github.com/dotnet/brand), which publishes its artwork under CC0. Original logo colors and proportions are retained. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; use here identifies the technologies, without implying endorsement.

Upstream SVGs and complete license notices are retained in `assets/vendor`. Themed versions in `assets/windows` wrap those unchanged paths in light/dark Windows-style tiles. README uses separate images so stack icons can wrap on small screens.
